## Field source and timing rule

Use **the H1 co-reported GSE mean vector** for each of the four initial scalar outputs: `B = hypot(BX,BY,BZ)`. It is already paired to the corresponding plasma spectrum and has matching measurement support. No separate field interpolation or maximum clock offset is needed for the primary pathway. Compute the vector magnitude **after** taking the source's component averages. `|⟨B⃗⟩|` must never be labelled `⟨|B⃗|⟩`; MFI `B3F1` explicitly reports the latter kind of average. `Ang_dev` and `dev` are retained as descriptive field-variation context, with no invented cutoff.

`WI_H0_MFI` becomes preferable when the research question needs a separately audited field, raw three-second variation, or a sensitivity comparison; any spectrum analysis is beyond this pass. For a future same-support comparison, choose `B3GSE`, `Epoch3`, `NUM3_PTS`, **not** one-minute nearest-neighbour `BGSE` and not `B3F1`. MFI `Epoch3` is documented as centered on its three-second average. Pair a nominal bin `[Epoch3−1.5 s, Epoch3+1.5 s)` with the nominal SWE support `[Epoch,Epoch+92 s)` by exact temporal overlap; form the overlap-duration-weighted mean of the three **components**, then take its magnitude. Require complete 92-second coverage by non-fill MFI bins with `NUM3_PTS>0`, no duplicated bins, and no uncovered time; thus maximum allowed uncovered support is **0 s**, with no nearest-neighbour offset or interpolation. Report raw contributing-bin count and coverage for each pair. This is a conditional future comparison rule, not a claim that H1 and H0 values are bitwise identical. Exact individual SWE integration durations beyond the archived nominal 92 seconds are not encoded in these CDF variables; that limits use of this optional pairing for sub-spectrum physical claims and is listed below as open.

## Frozen processing contract

1. Read the identified **versioned** CDF, verify its SHA-256, `Logical_source`, version, `Epoch` and variable attributes, then convert CDF epoch to ISO UTC. A different file or version requires a new manifest and reinspection. Select rows by **spectrum start** in `[16:00,18:00)` UTC as above. Do not bin to a regular grid.
2. Keep a source-row ID (file name plus zero-based CDF record index) and source timestamp. Treat duplicate or non-monotone epochs as rejected input; do not reorder without recording it. Maintain a separate count for malformed-time rows that cannot enter the window selection.
3. For each selected row retain only `fit_flag=10`, as [archive flag documentation](https://cdaweb.gsfc.nasa.gov/misc/NotesW.html) calls this solar-wind parameters OK. Reject other codes with their exact code. Codes concerning alpha fits are **not** automatically equivalent to 10 for these proton variables; a broader policy is a later sensitivity decision. The source's own solar-wind/fit bounds already screen some records; no second chi-squared or field-deviation cutoff is invented.
4. Required values are `Proton_Np_nonlin`, `Proton_W_nonlin`, `Proton_Wperp_nonlin`, and all three of `BX/BY/BZ`. Reject any archive fill, non-finite value, value outside its CDF `VALIDMIN/VALIDMAX`, `n_p≤0`, either required thermal speed `≤0`, or `|B|≤0`. These strict-positive conditions follow the existing formula domains. Reject mismatched or missing units/frame metadata instead of guessing. An absent optional flow, alpha, fit uncertainty, parallel speed, or field-deviation field is recorded as unavailable but does not invalidate the four initial outputs.
5. Preserve each archive value and each conversion in the trace. Convert `n_p` cm⁻³ → m⁻³ and `B⃗` nT → T; compute `B=|⟨B⃗⟩|`. Use [Kasper et al. (2006)](https://wind.nasa.gov/docs/Kasper_SWE_Calibration_PaperJGR2006.pdf) and the [archive trace definition](https://cdaweb.gsfc.nasa.gov/misc/NotesW.html): `T_trace[eV]=m_p(1000 W_scalar[km/s])²/(2e)` for **proton trace beta**, and `T_perp[eV]=m_p(1000 W_perp[km/s])²/(2e)` for **perpendicular-temperature proton gyroradius**. The core uses `sqrt(kT/m)`, so its latter thermal speed equals the archived perpendicular `W/√2`. Do not pass `W` as a temperature or silently replace `T_perp` with `T_trace`. If `Wpar` is present, record a trace-relation check; no undocumented tolerance or extra rejection follows from it in this pilot.
6. Call only the existing `plasma-physics.js` functions listed below, once per retained row, with `Z=1`, `mu=1`, and proton-only mass density for Alfvén speed. No alpha abundance is assumed. Save each canonical input, output, unit, formula ID, warning, source-row ID, and assumption. An exception or non-finite output rejects that **output** with reason; it does not become zero. Treat model applicability separately from input validity and from arithmetic success.
7. No interpolation, replacement, smoothing, averaging of plasma rows, background subtraction, or cross-product joining occurs in the primary path. A sample's scalar outputs use that sample's converted inputs; the interval summary is calculated from the **per-sample outputs**, never by evaluating a formula on averaged inputs. Preserve observed gaps; report start-to-start separations and uncovered nominal support `max(0, next Epoch−(Epoch+92 s))`. Use timestamped markers in the figure, with no connecting line that could conceal gaps. No background interval is required for these absolute quantities.
8. Account for `N_selected`, `N_retained`, `N_rejected` and a mutually exclusive **first-failure** reason in this order: invalid/duplicate time, disallowed fit code, required fill/missing/non-finite, required archive range, physical non-positive, metadata mismatch, calculation failure. Also retain all detected reasons per row. The primary observed preliminary inventory is **73 selected, 73 retained, 0 rejected** under required-field and flag checks; the candidate is 72 selected, 57 flag-10 candidates, 15 disallowed flags. These counts must be recomputed in implementation, with any difference explained.
9. Minimum support for a **numerical row output** is one complete retained row. At zero rows, produce no value. A numerical median/min/max is defined for one or more retained rows, but no arbitrary number of rows certifies a scientifically representative interval; that interpretation remains outside this specification. The 73-row pilot exceeds the purely computational minimum.

**Historical initial table plan:** one row per quantity `n_p`, `|⟨B⃗⟩|`, `T_trace,p`, `T_perp,p`, `β_p,trace`, `d_p`, `ρ_p,perp,σ`, and `v_A,p-only`; columns are unit, retained count, median (ordinary midpoint median for even counts), minimum, maximum, measurement source, and formula/convention. Add interval UTC, SWE file/version/hash, retained/rejected totals, and warning counts as table metadata. Each statistic is over per-spectrum values; report no total plasma beta. The implemented bundle's summary and plotting data are described in the [workbench bundle](workbench_bundle.md).

**Historical initial figure plan:** four aligned panels of `β_p,trace` (dimensionless), `d_p` (km), `ρ_p,perp,σ` (km), and `v_A,p-only` (km s⁻¹) against SWE spectrum-start UTC, each as discrete markers and with retained count. Display no interpolated segments or mode/instability labels. Plotting data must carry source-row IDs. The implemented bundle instead contains rowwise plotting data and a numerical two-scale SVG; see the [workbench bundle](workbench_bundle.md).

## Four-formula dependency and evidence map

All four formulas consume positive finite inputs and the source time pairing above; a correct value only establishes numerical evaluation of the declared inputs. The CDF flags/units determine **input validity**. The model statements below determine **applicability**. `validation.js` and `FORMULA_AUDIT.md` distinguish **evidence**; interpretation is separate again. Internal canonical density is m⁻³, field T, temperature energy-equivalent eV. Output display conversion to km or km s⁻¹ happens after calculation. None of the four scalar equations requires a velocity-frame transform; record GSE for provenance and future vector use.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proton **trace** beta / `species-beta` | `speciesBeta(n_p_m3,T_trace_eV,B_T)` → dimensionless; `2 μ₀ n_p e T_trace[eV]/B²`. | Species H⁺ pressure only, trace scalar from anisotropic fit; no electron/alpha pressure. `validation.js` has a beta-pressure **internal restatement** (`F_REGRESSION`), unit and smoke checks; no beta-specific external numerical benchmark. | Independently calculate `p_p=n_p m_p W_scalar²/2` and `p_B=B²/(2μ₀)` from source row in separate reference arithmetic, then compare `p_p/p_B`. Confirm temperature conversion and source units first. |
| Proton inertial length / `ion-inertial-length` | `ionInertialLength(n_p_m3,1,1)` → m; `c/sqrt(n_p e²/(ε₀m_p))`. | One proton species, charge +e, mass mₚ; no temperature or B. The [NRL Plasma Formulary (2023), p. 28](https://www.nrl.navy.mil/Portals/38/PDF%20Files/NRL_Plasma_Formulary_2023.pdf) gives the proton inertial, gyroradius and Alfvén speed definitions/coefficient context; exact unrounded agreement requires matched constants and conventions. Regression, analytical identity, dimensional/unit, domain/property, source-provenance, and independent external/reference checks must be reported as different evidence classes. File hashes establish provenance, not correctness. The public [Wind MFI/SWE plot service](https://wind.nasa.gov/mfi_swe_plot.php) includes proton beta and Alfvén velocity and may be contextual comparison; it does not certify an exact expected series because processing choices can differ.

The existing v1.1.0 formula audit and decision log therefore remain unchanged.
