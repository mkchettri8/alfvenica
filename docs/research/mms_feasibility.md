# MMS transfer feasibility — contract required before implementation

**No MMS interval, file version, checksum, DOI or derived result has been selected or verified for Alfvenica.** This brief identifies a possible next *data contract*, not MMS support and not a claim that Wind validation transfers to the magnetosphere.

| Required decision | Candidate and evidence to inspect before a contract is frozen |
| --- | --- |
| Products and observatory | A bounded **MMS1** candidate could combine FPI Level-2 fast-survey `dis-moms` and `des-moms` with FGM Level-2 survey vectors. The [MMS Science Data Center](https://lasp.colorado.edu/mms/sdc/public/datasets/) lists FPI and FIELDS as distinct instruments, and its [FPI](https://lasp.colorado.edu/mms/sdc/public/datasets/fpi/) and [FIELDS](https://lasp.colorado.edu/mms/sdc/public/datasets/fields/) pages describe the products. Select an actual interval, file names, versions, checksums, archive citations and DOI identifiers only after coverage/quality review. Do not infer other spacecraft from MMS1. |
| Frames and variables | FPI moments include GSE bulk velocity and GSE pressure/temperature tensors as well as DBCS variants in the [FPI products guide](https://spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov/pub/data/mms/documents/FPI_dpg.pdf). FGM survey offers `mms1_fgm_b_gse_srvy_l2` in nT; the [FGM guide](https://spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov/pub/data/mms/documents/FGM_DataProductsGuide2016Apr20.pdf) identifies its first three entries as GSE vector components and fourth as magnitude. A future contract must name exact CDF variables and verify frame metadata per file. Tensor projection along B is a separate defined operation; a scalar temperature is not automatically `T_perp`. |
| Cadence and time support | FPI fast-survey ion/electron moments and FGM survey samples have different supports. The [FPI release notes](https://lasp.colorado.edu/mms/sdc/public/datasets/fpi/mms-FPIReleaseNotes-220217.pdf) discuss 4.5-s fast-survey maps; the [FGM guide](https://spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov/pub/data/mms/documents/FGM_DataProductsGuide2016Apr20.pdf) describes survey 8/16 samples s⁻¹ and a `bdeltahalf` support field. Verify each selected file's epoch interpretation, integration boundaries, clock corrections, gaps and mode changes. Define vector averaging and count support before any pairing; do not import Wind's nominal 92-s or H1 co-reporting rule. |
| Data quality | Inspect FPI quality-flag bit meanings, spacecraft-potential/photoelectron corrections, low counts and fitted-moment caveats in the [FPI guide](https://spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov/pub/data/mms/documents/FPI_dpg.pdf) and current release notes. Inspect FGM flags/range/mode in the FGM guide. Freeze sample exclusions by instrument and species; never reuse Wind `fit_flag=10`. FPI and FGM revisions can change quality and calibration. |
| Species and model | FPI ion moments require a verified species interpretation; do not assume pure H⁺ or use a proton-only mass density without an explicit supported approximation. Electron moments could support an electron pressure term only after tensor/scalar conventions and co-timing are established. MMS regimes may require composition, anisotropy, spacecraft-frame transformations, pressure-tensor projection and different applicability checks. No automatic KAW, Walén, turbulence or reconnection verdict follows from the Wind four-scalar chain. |
| Validation | Acquire independently checked source-to-variable mappings, analytic/unit tests for frame and tensor transformations, gap/flag refusals, at least one external numerical comparator under matching definitions, covariance/uncertainty semantics and an outside MMS specialist review. Run a pilot interval and a contrasting stress case before claiming transfer. Preserve exact file/data lineage and separate numerical replay from physical validation. |

**Decision:** do not build an MMS adapter or analysis until a Pass-1-quality MMS specification resolves the open rows above and a real interval is chosen for a question it can answer. The candidate products identify where to investigate, not a validated MMS workflow.
